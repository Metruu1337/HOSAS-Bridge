# Where to share

Consider WARDOGS groups (including Polish communities), HOTAS/HOSAS, flight-sim, space-sim and controller/remapping communities. Pick relevant channels where members actually discuss this problem.

Read each community's self-promotion, open-source/project-posting and general rules first. Ask moderators if unclear. Use the real repository/release links and exact tested scope; disclose unsigned binaries and beta limitations. Invite specific compatibility feedback. Avoid duplicate cross-post spam or unsolicited messages. Nothing is posted automatically by the project or this preparation.
